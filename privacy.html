Privacy Policy

App: Softly Calc  |  Developer: Softly Apps  |  Effective date: May 4, 2026

We collect nothing

Softly Calc does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no account required.

What stays on your device

The app saves three things locally on your device only — your chosen theme, your mute preference, and your calculation history. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

Softly Calc uses no third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children

This app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
